01 / CONTACTS

Know who you are trading with.

Keep company details, Tax ID and verification status together on the Odoo contact card.

  • One-click VAT and EORI checks using your configured VIESAC account.
  • Clear status badges distinguish confirmed, invalid, pending and unverified results.
  • Registry details include the audit reference and a consultation number when the authority returns one.
02 / AUDIT ARCHIVE

Keep the result and its evidence together.

Open the audit behind a transaction without leaving Odoo.

  • View the checked identifier, source, timestamp and authority consultation number.
  • Open available PDF certificates and XML evidence from the audit record. Certificate PDFs support 23 VIESAC API locales.
  • Audit access follows allowed companies. SHA-256 evidence hashes are not digital signatures.
03 / INVOICES

Give every invoice its own audit context.

Keep audit records and available certificates linked to the accounting document.

  • Customer invoices can reuse an eligible linked sales-order audit.
  • Invoice verification details come from its linked audit, rather than the contact’s later status.
  • Supplier bills support warnings or strict rejection of invalid registrations. Tax eligibility remains an accounting decision.
04 / SALES & DELIVERY

Control delivery while an audit is pending.

Apply the delivery-hold policy when an order audit is pending or provisionally limited.

  • The order shows the customer VAT status and its related audit records.
  • When a VIESAC hold applies, outgoing stock completion is blocked.
  • Successful verification through scheduled polling or a signed webhook releases the hold.

A daily rates catalog

Browse standard and reduced VAT/GST rates for 115 catalog countries. Add a company standard sales tax or review EU destination rates.

Background updates

Odoo refreshes open audits every 10 minutes and synchronizes rates daily. Optional timestamped HMAC webhooks can trigger an immediate refresh.

Coverage

European registries. Global reach.

The connector uses the shared VIESAC API. Registry availability and the depth of verification vary by jurisdiction; a supported format or a published rate is not proof of active VAT registration.

  • EU VIES: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden; plus Northern Ireland (XI).
  • National registry routes: UK HMRC, Norway BRREG, Swiss UID, Liechtenstein MWST, Australia ABR, Turkey GİB, Serbia PURS, UAE FTA, Singapore IRAS, Japan NTA, India GSTN, South Africa SARS, Argentina, Kenya KRA, Oman, Egypt, Taiwan, Albania, Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Iceland and North Macedonia.
  • Additional rate and format coverage: Algeria, Andorra, Azerbaijan, Bahamas, Bahrain, Bangladesh, Barbados, Bolivia, Botswana, Cambodia, Canada, Cape Verde, Chile, China, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, El Salvador, Ghana, Guatemala, Honduras, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Israel, Ivory Coast, Jamaica, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan, Lebanon, Malaysia, Mauritius, Mexico, Moldova, Mongolia, Montenegro, Morocco, Namibia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Qatar, Rwanda, San Marino, Saudi Arabia, Senegal, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Tajikistan, Tanzania, Thailand, Trinidad and Tobago, Tunisia, Ukraine, Uruguay, Uzbekistan and Vietnam.
