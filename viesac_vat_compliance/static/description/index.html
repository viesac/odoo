How VIESAC Integrates Across Your Odoo Modules

👤

1. Customers & Vendors (Contacts)

Instantly validate European and international VAT/GST and EORI numbers directly on partner cards. Whenever a tax identifier is entered or updated:

  • Instant Status Badges: Clear indicators (VALID (VIES), VALID (HMRC), VALID (BRREG), etc.) showing exact authority confirmation.
  • Official Consultation Numbers: Unique VIES consultation identifier stamped directly on the contact record.
  • One-Click Audit: Dedicated Check VAT and Check EORI header buttons for immediate on-demand audits.
  • Full Audit Trail in Chatter: Date, timestamp and official PDF/XML proof logged permanently into the partner chatter.
🧾

2. Invoices & Accounting (Account Move)

Legally substantiate intra-community 0% VAT (Reverse Charge) and cross-border tax exemptions on customer invoices:

  • Statutory PDF Footnote: Automatically prints official VIES consultation numbers, verification timestamps, and legal reference (Art. 138 EU Directive 2006/112/EC) on invoice PDF printouts.
  • Automatic Certificate Attachments: Official PDF Audit Certificates in 23 EU languages and tamper-evident SHA-256 XML evidence attached directly to invoice records.
  • Tax Audit Defense: Complete legal evidence bundle ready for immediate submission during tax authority inspections.
📦

3. Sales Orders & Warehouse Dispatch (Delivery Holds)

Protect your company from fraudulent cross-border orders before goods leave the warehouse:

  • Automated Delivery Hold: If an order is confirmed but the customer's VAT check is pending or unverified, outbound warehouse delivery orders (stock.picking) are automatically put on hold.
  • Warehouse Guard: Warehouse dispatchers are blocked from validating shipments until tax authority verification succeeds.
  • Auto-Release: Once the scheduled background job or webhook confirms valid status, the delivery hold is released automatically.
🛡️

4. Vendor Bills & Supplier Risk Control

Eliminate the risk of tax authorities denying input VAT deduction due to invalid or deregistered suppliers:

  • Configurable Vendor Bill Policy: Choose between friendly warning notifications or strict blocking when attempting to post vendor bills with unverified or invalid supplier VAT registrations.
  • Deregistration Alerts: Catch suppliers whose VAT registrations were canceled or revoked by tax offices.
📈

5. Statutory Tax Rates Catalog & OSS Support

Synchronize official statutory VAT and GST rates across 115+ countries worldwide:

  • Daily Automatic Catalog Sync: Always up-to-date standard and reduced rates for all EU member states and global jurisdictions.
  • One-Click Standard Tax Creation: Easily generate compliant standard sales/purchase tax definitions in your company chart of accounts.
  • OSS Destination Review: Inspect European destination rates for clean cross-border B2C e-commerce reporting.

Easy Setup in 3 Steps

  1. Install the Module: Add viesac_vat_compliance to your Odoo addons path and install via the Apps menu.
  2. Configure API Key: Navigate to Accounting → Configuration → Settings → VIESAC Compliance. Enter your API key from viesac.eu/app/api and click Test Connection.
  3. Choose Policies: Select your preferred PDF certificate language (from 23 official locales), enable automatic attachments, delivery holds, or vendor bill controls.
