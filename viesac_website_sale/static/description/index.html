Automate intra-EU B2B zero-rating and cross-border VAT validation on your Odoo eCommerce web store. Instant verification with European Commission VIES and national tax registries directly at checkout.

Seamless B2B Checkout Experience

🛒

Automated Real-Time Validation on Billing Address

When an enterprise buyer selects company checkout and inputs their European or international VAT registration:

  • Instant Feedback: The system verifies the VAT number with European Commission VIES or the relevant national registry in real time.
  • Automated 0% VAT Recalculation: If the B2B customer is eligible for cross-border Intra-Community supply, Odoo fiscal positions immediately switch the cart total to 0% Reverse Charge.
  • Anti-Scraping & Session Cache: Verified numbers are cached per cart session with strict rate-limiting, preventing malicious automated scraping.

Key eCommerce Benefits

⚡

Instant B2B Tax Recalculation

Valid EU VAT numbers trigger automatic fiscal position recalculation in Odoo, applying 0% Intra-Community VAT (Reverse Charge) to eligible B2B buyers smoothly and reliably.

🛡️

Anti-Abuse & Harvesting Protection

Built-in cart session throttling and cryptographic response validation prevent malicious bots from using your checkout as a free VAT scraping tool.

🚀

Fast In-Session Cache

Verified VAT numbers are cached for the checkout session, avoiding duplicate API calls and providing lightning-fast page navigation for your customers.

📑

Connected Back-Office Audits

Orders placed through the web store link directly to the back-office audit trail in viesac_vat_compliance, storing official consultation numbers and evidence certificates.
